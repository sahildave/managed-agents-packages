# Content ideas

The agent instructions say what to do each run. This guide fixes what makes a good source and the list layout.

## Source rules
- Good sources: Reddit threads, Hacker News comments, Indie Hackers posts, Stack Exchange questions, GitHub discussions, public X or Bluesky posts. Use threads from the last 14 days where possible and show the date.
- Each idea needs at least one real question with a link. An idea without a source question is a guess; leave it out.
- "Unanswered well" means the top replies are wrong, vague, outdated or only promote a product.
- Do not quote more than one short sentence from a post. Never copy someone's post as the idea.
- Skip ideas whose source question or angle is already in memory.

## List layout
```text
# Ten content ideas, week of <yyyy-mm-dd>

1. <Working title>
   - Source question: "<short quote>" (<link>, <date>)
   - Angle: <what you can say that the replies did not>
   - Fits: <platform> as <thread, post, guide, video>
   - Ties to your product: <yes, how / no>
2. ...

## Sources I could not reach
- <site>: <reason>
```
Rank by usefulness to the reader first, product fit second. Save each idea's title and source link to memory.
