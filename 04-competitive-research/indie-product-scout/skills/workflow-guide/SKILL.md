# Launch brief

The agent instructions say what to do each run. This guide fixes which sources count and the brief layout.

## Sources
- Launch sources: Product Hunt, Show HN on Hacker News, Indie Hackers, BetaList, and relevant subreddits. Record which ones you could reach.
- The launch date is the date on the launch post. If you cannot see a date, say "date not shown" and leave the product out of "this week".
- Positioning comes from the product's own site (headline, pricing page, who it says it is for), not from the launch post's comments.
- Upvotes, comments and "trending" badges are attention, not traction. Never report revenue or user counts unless the founder published them; then quote and link the source.
- A product already in memory is not new. Mention it again only if its site changed in a way that matters.

## Report layout
```text
# New in <niche>, week of <yyyy-mm-dd>

## Worth a look
### <Product> (<launch source>, <launch date>)
- Site: <URL>   Launch post: <URL>
- Says it is for: "<short quote from their site>"
- Pricing: <as shown, or "not shown">
- Versus you: <one or two sentences, marked as inference>

## Also launched (lower relevance)
- <Product>: <URL>, <one line>

## Sources I could not reach
- <source>: <reason>

## Next actions
1. <action>
```
Save each product's name, URLs and launch date to memory so later runs skip it.
